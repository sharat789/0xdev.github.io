Hey there! I'm Sharat, a DevOps engineer who spends way too much time tweaking configs and arguing with YAML.

This blog is my digital garden—where I document the chaos of building software, the occasional victory, and everything in between.

## What I do

I work primarily with cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and making sure deployments don't happen on Fridays (most of the time). Think Kubernetes, Nginx, and a healthy amount of shell scripts that somehow work despite looking like ancient hieroglyphics.

## Why this blog

I started this blog for three reasons:

1. **To remember things** — Because I'll definitely forget how I solved that weird Docker networking issue in 6 months
2. **To share knowledge** — If I spent 4 hours debugging something, maybe someone else can benefit from that pain
3. **To improve my writing** — Explaining things forces you to understand them better

## What you'll find here

- Tutorials that worked for me (your mileage may vary)
- Rants about tooling that should be simpler
- Side projects that may or may not be abandoned
- Occasional hot takes on tech

## The stack

This blog runs on Hugo because I wanted something fast and I was too lazy to maintain a React-based site.
