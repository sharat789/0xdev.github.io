## How I Got Here

I got into the micro-blogging space 2 years ago when I first started my job as a Frontend Platform Engineer. It all began when I noticed my colleague casually browsing [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) while sipping his morning coffee—which quickly became my ritual too.

I was impressed by how everyday developers had their own blog websites where they shared knowledge and experience with the world, often with a tinge of humor and sarcasm that made technical content a very nice addition to your daily monotonous task of asking people to rebase their PR when shit doesn't work.

## What I Do

I'm currently part of a team that manages the platform for all frontend teams in our company. Our focus is on creating a space where developers can just write code without worrying about the CI/CD process—given they follow the rules and constraints we've set (almost never happens).

The key areas I thoroughly enjoy in this role include:

- **System Design**: Architecting solutions for an ever-increasing customer base.
- **Maintainability**: Thinking about long-term code health and developer experience.
- **Scalability**: Discussing how solutions will (or won't) scale gracefully.

## What I Hope to Achieve

With this blog, I hope to accomplish several things:

1. **Better Problem Solving**: Forcing myself to think more about certain solutions so that I get more content to share here as every individual writes garbage code but they get to know about it when other people see it.
2. **Improved Communication**: Improve my writing and documentation skills (the ultimate irreplaceable corporate weapon, as we all know).
3. **Consistent Learning**: Maybe when I see the last commit was 6 months ago, a fire will light under my ass to finally finish that project for a new post.
